A frog is a tailless amphibian with a short squat body, moist smooth skin, and very long hind legs for leaping.Frogs are known for their moist, permeable skin, lack of tails, and powerful hind legs for leaping, living on every continent except Antarctica.Frogs are diverse, tailless amphibians found worldwide (except Antarctica) with over 7,000 species.

Frogs cannot live in Antarctica because it is too cold and dry for them to survive and reproduce.Adult frogs are primarily carnivores that eat a wide variety of live, moving prey, including insects,spiders, worms, slugs, and snails. frogs are heavily dependent animals, primarily relying on moist, aquatic, or semi-aquatic habitats for survival and reproduction.
